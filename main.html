Overview


Cybercrime has been a global issue for years where it gradually evolves to make victims start doubting whether this contact, content, bank, or advertisements are true or false. Cybercrime has been tearing apart businesses by impersonation them and such.


Cybercrime is an illegal activity and should not be performed for entertainment purposes only and should be taken as a serious crime. Performing this activity risks everything around you just like a traditional crime activity. People should not encourage to perform cybercrimes after being a victim from it, but rather spread awareness to other people to avoid being a victim of cybercrime.


This site is focused to give awareness to individuals that has experienced, or want to avoid being a victim of cybercrimes, especially the Generation X.
